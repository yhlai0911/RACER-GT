# Code of Conduct · 行為準則

## English

### Our commitment

We want RACER-GT to be a place where a graduate student can point out that a
proposition is wrong and be thanked for it. Everything below follows from that.

### Expected behaviour

- Criticise methods, code, and claims directly. Substantive disagreement is the
  point of a research project.
- Separate the argument from the person making it.
- Assume the other party has a reason; ask for it before concluding they do not.
- Accept correction in public without treating it as a loss.

### Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning language about a contributor's
  identity, institution, seniority, or command of English or Chinese.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Dismissing a technical objection on the basis of who raised it.

### Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space
where someone is representing the project.

### Enforcement

Report concerns to **yhlai@mail.dyu.edu.tw**. Reports are handled
confidentially. Responses range from a private request to change behaviour,
through temporary restriction from interaction, to a permanent ban, in
proportion to severity and repetition. The maintainer is subject to this
document on the same terms as everyone else.

## 繁體中文

### 我們的承諾

我們希望 RACER-GT 是一個「研究生可以指出某條命題有誤、並因此被感謝」的地方。以下所有條文都由此而來。

### 期待的行為

- 直接批評方法、程式與主張。實質的意見不同正是研究專案的意義所在。
- 把論點與提出論點的人分開。
- 假定對方有其理由；在斷定沒有之前，先詢問。
- 公開接受更正，不將其視為挫敗。

### 不可接受的行為

- 人身攻擊、騷擾，或針對貢獻者的身分、機構、資歷、中英文能力的貶抑言語。
- 公開他人的私人資訊。
- 持續干擾討論。
- 以「是誰提出的」為由駁回技術性質疑。

### 適用範圍

適用於本 repository、其 issues 與 pull requests，以及任何有人代表本專案的場合。

### 執行

請將疑慮回報至 **yhlai@mail.dyu.edu.tw**，將以保密方式處理。回應依嚴重程度與是否再犯，可能為私下要求改變行為、暫時限制互動，或永久停權。維護者本人同樣受本文件約束，條件與所有人相同。
